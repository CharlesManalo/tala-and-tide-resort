Recommended activities

Plan a beach walk, a safe-weather paddle, or a guided coastal outing. Choose inland or pool activities if sea conditions are unsuitable; waterfalls and longer trips require local arrangements.

Stay & dining options

The concept offers Family Rooms and a Barkada Villa for overnight guests, with daytime cottages for gatherings. Shared coastal lunches and room breakfasts are sample dining options; ask about dietary needs.

Travel reminders

Northern coast travel can take time. Check road and weather advisories, carry cash for small vendors, and confirm local transport for your return.

Tourist Guide

Arrive informed. Travel with care.

Best time to visit

February–April is a useful planning window. A dry-season planning window for beach visits; sea conditions still require a daily check. This is seasonal guidance, not a weather guarantee.

Weather considerations

PAGASA describes a broad dry season from December to May and rainy season from June to November. December to February is cooler; March to May is hotter. Local rainfall varies. Check current PAGASA forecasts, tropical cyclone bulletins and marine warnings before outdoor trips.

What to bring

Pack reef-conscious sun protection, swimwear, a hat, water shoes, a refillable bottle, a dry bag, and a change of clothes.

Dress recommendations

Wear swimwear in swimming areas and a cover-up in shared dining spaces. Secure sandals or water shoes are useful on uneven shores.

Safety reminders

Check sea conditions before entering the water. Wear a life vest for guided activities, never swim alone, and supervise children closely.

Local customs

Respect fishing boats, working shore areas, and private homes. Ask before taking close-up photos of people.

Environmental guidelines

Take litter home, avoid stepping on coral, and leave shells and wildlife where they belong. Use refill stations and reusable containers.

Accessibility

The resort concept includes ground-floor room options. Sand and uneven coastal paths may limit wheelchair access; confirm step-free routes, bathroom features, and assistance first.

Estimated budget

Plan ₱350 per adult for a day pass or ₱4,800 per Family Room per night, plus around ₱700–₱1,200 per adult per day for food. Long-distance transport is extra; figures are estimates.

Tourist etiquette

Keep beach speakers at a considerate volume, follow pool rules, and ask before using shared cottages or sports equipment.

Emergency information

Dial 911

For life-threatening emergencies in the Philippines, call Unified 911. It coordinates police, fire, medical and rescue response. Tell the dispatcher your actual location and follow their instructions. Keep essential medication and an emergency contact accessible.
