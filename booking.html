A few useful answers
Before you make plans.
Is the sea always safe for swimming?
No. Sea conditions can change quickly. Follow guides and flags, stay in designated areas, and choose the pool if swimming is not advised.
What is the children’s sample rate?
For package calculator estimates, ages 4–11 are half the adult package rate; under 4s are not counted and share existing bedding. The final room capacity and price need confirmation.
Can we visit for the day?
Yes. The Beach Day Pass covers stated access and shared seating. Private cottages are a separate option.
Do you allow big groups?
The Barkada Villa concept fits 10–15 guests. Ask about bedding, meal counts, and the schedule before choosing it.
What happens in bad weather?
Water activities may be moved, replaced, or canceled for safety. Ask for the applicable cancellation and refund policy before confirming with a real operator.
Are pets welcome?
Pet-friendly spaces are by request. Share your pet’s size and needs in the message; acceptance would depend on house rules.
Can I pay or reserve here?
No. This academic website only prepares an inquiry until a real form service is connected. It does not take payments or confirm reservations.
