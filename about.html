Pagudpud · Beach resort

A place with a point of view.

A beach resort concept with a simple belief: the best part of a holiday is who you share it with.

Background

Built around togetherness.

Tala & Tide imagines an approachable beachfront retreat in Pagudpud, Ilocos Norte. Family rooms, group villas, and day-use cottages let different travelers find their own version of a beach day.

Target market

Who we welcome.

Families, groups of friends, and couples who want a relaxed beach break with easy-to-compare group options.

Mission

What guides us.

To make shared beach holidays easy, welcoming, and mindful of the coast.

Vision

Where we hope to go.

To inspire longer, more thoughtful visits to northern Ilocos through local experiences and responsible recreation.

History & development

From idea to invitation.

A fictional concept timeline created for this academic project.

2024

A beach-day idea

The original concept paired group-friendly stays with a transparent approach to holiday costs.

2025

Space for the whole crew

Family rooms, cottages, shared dining, and supervised activity plans were developed.

2026

A coastal welcome

This academic resort concept brings the stay, the itinerary, and the group estimate into one useful place.

Tourism significance

Connected to the place.

The resort concept gives travelers a reason to spend more time in the area, connect with local food, and include nearby northern Ilocos sights in their trip.
