## Manual and automated accessibility review

Reviewed desktop and 360px mobile screenshots; checked image/copy balance, wrapping, input sizing, and gallery/card readability. Axe checked all eight pages against WCAG 2 A/AA and WCAG 2.1 A/AA rules, in addition to keyboard interaction checks. This is not a certification or a complete assistive-technology audit.
